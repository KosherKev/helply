Brand Identity Guidelines

How Helply looks, sounds and behaves. Version 1.0. Helply is an AI customer service and support platform for businesses, and the brand is built on one idea: technology that brings people together.

Brand essence

Partnership and trust

The handshake is the crossbar of the H. Every interaction should feel reliable and accountable.

Seamless communication

The speech bubble holds everything. Support should feel like one continuous conversation.

Clarity and simplicity

No clutter for support teams or their customers. Say the useful thing plainly.

Voice

Confident, warm, specific. Use plain verbs and sentence case. Describe what the product does instead of selling it. Errors explain what happened and how to fix it, without apologizing.

Logo

The logo is a rounded chat bubble containing an H whose crossbar is a handshake. Use the vector files from the asset kit and never redraw it.

Primary lockup. Use first, everywhere there is room.

Stacked lockup. For square or narrow spaces, covers and social banners.

Mark alone. Avatars, favicons and compact UI headers.

Outline mark. Watermarks, blueprints and subtle backgrounds only.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the H in the wordmark on every side. Nothing enters this area: no text, edges or other logos.

Logo use

Use the full-color logo on white and Ice Blue. Use the on-navy version on Deep Navy. Use the white mark inside the blue app icon. For one-color print, use the navy mono mark.

Don’t

Color

Blue and white do the work. Use roughly 70% white or Ice Blue, 20% Deep Navy, 10% Helply Blue, and keep Sky Blue for small accents. Accessible text pairs: Deep Navy on white is 17.8:1, Helply Blue on white and white on Helply Blue are both 5.2:1 (AA).

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans for everything: wordmark, headings, interface and documents. Fall back to Inter, then system sans-serif.

Use sentence case. Keep lines under 75 characters. Letter-spacing is -2% on display sizes and 0 elsewhere.

Website and product UI

Interfaces are white and Ice Blue with Deep Navy text. Helply Blue marks the one primary action on screen. Corners are rounded to echo the chat bubble: 12 px on controls, 20 px on cards and the chat widget, fully round on badges.
